# Appearance integration for the recovery candidate

The eight shipping GLBs reproduce the recorded v4b asset hashes. The exact generator is generate_animated_assets.py; current identity and geometry costs are in refined-manifest.json and delivery-manifest.json. Editable Blender files are regenerated equivalents, not recovered original container bytes. The original combined checkpoint, original native container bytes and original v4 preview/GIF archive remain unavailable. Root contact sheets are older v3 evidence.

Animals have rigid articulated nodes rather than skinned deformation. Five authored body clips and three wing clips are wired into the cosmetic runtime. Keep roots, socket and hinges fixed; preserve node names, animation timing and cached-resource ownership. These assets must not change collision, movement, scoring or network authority. Better skinned model selection remains a separate open quality task.

Blender 4.3.2 generation repeated byte-exactly. Native re-export comparison and all 28 clips sampled at 121 poses pass Three r180 validation. These are source/import checks, not GPU/FPS or target-device acceptance. See README.md and docs/readiness/RENDER-PROFILING-ACCEPTANCE.md for remaining gates.
